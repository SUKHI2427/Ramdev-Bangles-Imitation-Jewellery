Our story

A neighbourhood shop that grew with the neighbourhood.

Ramdev Bangles & Imitation Jewellery opened its doors in 2011 on Kothanur Dinne Main Road, and has been part of JP Nagar 7th Phase ever since — more than fourteen years of bangles for brides, festivals, school functions and Tuesday evenings.

We stock gold-plated bangles and imitation jewellery in every budget, and we rent bridal and occasion sets for the days that need a little more sparkle. Walk in, try things on, and take your time.
