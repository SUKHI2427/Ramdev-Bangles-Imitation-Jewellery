Our Heritage

A Landmark Jewellery Destination in JP Nagar 7th Phase.

Established in 2011 on Kothanur Dinne Main Road, Ramdev Bangles & Imitation Jewellery has blossomed into one of South Bengaluru's most trusted fashion jewelry superstores. For over fourteen years, we have adorned brides, college students, dance troupes, and families for their most treasured milestones.

What began as an intimate bangle counter has grown into an expansive showroom featuring eight dedicated aisles. Whether you are seeking micro gold-plated daily-wear kadas, hundreds of festival glass bangles, or full bridal temple jewellery suites on rent for weddings and receptions, our family is here to guide you with personal warmth and fair pricing.
